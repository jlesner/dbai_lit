# Tag <span class="tag big">labels</span>

**Benchmark ground truth**: audits that measure how often a benchmark's labels, tests or verifiers are wrong (false accepts or false rejects of the grading), and benchmarks or pipelines whose labels a check backs (proofs, certified counterexamples, executable specifications).
