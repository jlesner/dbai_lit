# Tag <span class="tag big">gym</span>

**Generators of checkable tasks**: procedural generators of tasks whose answers a program computes and checks (environments, puzzle or program suites), released as a resource, or audits of such generators' checks.

- **[Unified Multi-Dimensional Benchmark for Complex Graph Reasoning in Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.12391 "Unified Multi-Dimensional Benchmark for Complex Graph Reasoning in Large Language Models (2026)")** (preprint 2026).
