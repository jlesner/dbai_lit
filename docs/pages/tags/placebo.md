# Tag <span class="tag big">placebo</span>

**Does the gain depend on the feedback's content?** The item's own results test whether a system's gain or decision depends on its feedback's content, by an intervention that keeps the feedback's form and breaks its link to inputs (shuffled, reassigned or constant verdicts).
