# Tag <span class="tag big">compact</span>

**Compact against frontier models**: the item's own results compare compact models (small, quantized or low-cost tiers), helped by inference or a harness or not, with larger ones on checked tasks, or measure what compression does to success on checked tasks.

<!-- filter -->

## Challenges

- **[Reliability that holds with size, by handing the procedure to an engine](#/challenges/engine_offloading_with_size)**: When a compact model writes a DSL program and an engine runs it, does its success rate hold as tasks grow, in data and in composition, and does the model know when the engine is the right tool?
- **[Prompt-space self-play judged by an engine](#/challenges/prompt_space_self_play)**: Can a proposer–solver loop with no labels, judged only by a DSL's engine, teach a compact model through what it writes into prompts (a skill document and a library of verified examples), and does it matter whether the compact model or a frontier model writes them?
- **[Gains made against a checker, carried beyond it](#/challenges/transfer_beyond_the_checker)**: Do gains a model makes against a program checker, in its weights or with its weights fixed (prompts, skills, memory), carry over to tasks the checker can't check: near, the same DSL on a task only examples check, such as writing a program from English; far, open-ended work in other domains?
- **[Learning a rarely seen DSL in prompts](#/challenges/unseen_dsl_in_prompts)**: Can a compact model learn a DSL that is rare in its training data, K through the open-source ngn/k engine, from the language's documentation and its engine alone, with what it learns kept in prompts?
