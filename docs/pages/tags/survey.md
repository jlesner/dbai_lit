- **[SoK: A Literature and Engineering Review of Regular Expression Denial of Service (ReDoS)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.11618 "SoK: A Literature and Engineering Review of Regular Expression Denial of Service (ReDoS) (2025)")** (ASIA CCS 2025). - **[Position: Don't Use the CLT in LLM Evals With Fewer Than a Few Hundred Datapoints](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.01747 "Position: Don't Use the CLT in LLM Evals With Fewer Than a Few Hundred Datapoints (2025)")** (ICML 2025 (spotlight position paper)). - **[Self-Play Only Evolves When Self-Synthetic Pipeline Ensures Learnable Information Gain](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.02218 "Self-Play Only Evolves When Self-Synthetic Pipeline Ensures Learnable Information Gain (2026)")** ((position paper), ICML 2026 (position track)).
