# Tag <span class="tag big">rlvr</span>

**Model weights are trained on a programmatic signal** (execution match, prover verdict, measured latency): reinforcement learning, including self-play and proposer–solver loops, and also rejection fine-tuning, expert iteration or STaR on outputs that pass a programmatic check, and preference losses whose labels come from a program.

## Challenges

- **[Self-improvement that compounds](#/challenges/compounding_self_improvement)**: Self-improving LLM loops gain in the first round, then stall or regress. Make the gains compound: each round adds certified information, and each change is kept only if a certified, statistically controlled check shows it doesn't regress.
- **[Weak checkers get exploited](#/challenges/weak_checker_exploitation)**: When a loop selects or trains on a checker's verdict, the checker's false accepts become targets. The harder the loop optimizes, the more answers it finds that the checker accepts but that are wrong.
