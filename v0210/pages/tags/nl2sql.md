## Challenges

- **[The self-improving SQL ↔ text ↔ verify loop](#/challenges/sql_text_round_trip_loop)**: Improve a describer (SQL→English), a writer (English→SQL) and an equivalence checker together, using certified checker verdicts as the reward.
- **[Text-to-SQL can't be verified against intent](#/challenges/text_to_sql_verification)**: English is ambiguous and gold queries are often wrong, so text-to-SQL is used here as a *source of pairs*, not as a goal.
